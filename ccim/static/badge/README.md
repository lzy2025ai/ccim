# Badge materials

The paper and clasp were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then exported as small WebP assets. The clasp has an SVG alpha silhouette to remove residual background glow. These are decorative materials only; employee portraits, names, status, navigation and controls remain live DOM. The SVG leather and corner textures are authored assets. Total runtime decoration payload: 80,786 bytes before transfer compression.

## Paper prompt

Create a seamless flat material texture swatch, square, warm ivory cream white premium embossed bookbinding paper. Very fine natural paper grain, subtle blind embossed flowing contour curves in a few areas, delicate irregular sweeping guilloche arcs engraved in the paper, extremely low contrast ivory on ivory, realistic grazing light from upper left showing recessed and raised fibers. No objects, no card boundary, no text, no letters, no logos, no diagonal repeating stripes. Entire image filled with same light cream paper, neutral brightness. Used behind readable dark UI text, texture must be quiet and pale.

## Clasp prompt

Create a single photorealistic luxury employee badge lanyard clasp isolated on genuinely transparent background. Straight front orthographic view, vertical dark navy blue leather strap entering from top, small rectangular polished antique gold buckle at lower end, thick rounded gold metal with bright specular edges, dark bronze inner recesses, cylindrical gold roller across bottom, real blue leather grain and stitching. Clasp should fill central 65% of image width and 90% height, no other objects, no text, no badge card, no logo, no shadows outside the object except subtle contact shadow beneath metal. Product photography, warm light upper left. This is a reusable small UI ornament, edges must be crisp.

## Clasp refinement prompt

Edit this ornament. Keep ONLY top navy leather strap and rectangular gold roller buckle. DELETE the entire lobster hook and swivel below rectangular buckle. Also DELETE all black/brown glow background and all outer glow. Genuine transparent alpha background outside leather and buckle AND inside buckle opening. Crop composition to strap and rectangular buckle only, front view, strap cut off flush at image top. The gold roller is the bottommost object. Compact badge clasp like a luxury ID card clip. Preserve photoreal leather grain and polished gold material. No new objects. Image should be tightly framed with only 5% empty margin.
